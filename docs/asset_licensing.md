# Asset licensing

The codebase does not include the local Cessna FBX because no accompanying
license, attribution, source URL, or redistribution terms were found. The model
must not be published unless the copyright holder's terms are identified and
confirmed compatible with redistribution.

Users may supply their own licensed model at
`assets/aircraft/cessna/cessna.fbx`. The primitive replay aircraft remains the
redistributable fallback and is sufficient for CI.
